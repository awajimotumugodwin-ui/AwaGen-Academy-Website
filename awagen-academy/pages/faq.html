Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for admission?

Visit Admissions, review the requirements and complete the demonstration application form. Production processing requires a backend.

What classes are available?

The interface supports Junior Secondary and Senior Secondary admissions; exact class availability should be configured by the school.

How are fees handled?

Fees are presented as information in the front-end. Secure payment processing requires a payment gateway and backend.

What student activities are available?

Clubs, sports, leadership, arts, competitions and educational trips are represented in the School Life section.

Is transportation available?

Transportation is represented as a school service; routes, pricing and availability can be connected later.

Is boarding available?

Boarding is listed as an optional service area and should be configured only if offered by the actual school.

How do portals work?

The portal pages are demonstration interfaces. Authentication, databases and permissions require a backend.

How can I contact the school?

Use the Contact page and the demonstration phone/email details, which must be replaced with verified information before publication.
